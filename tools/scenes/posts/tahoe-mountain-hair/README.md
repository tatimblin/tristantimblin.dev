# tahoe-mountain-hair

The post art for *Tahoe Mountain Hair*: a module sheet. On the left, the one
strong element — the water cycle as five stage rings on a dotted orbit about
an H2O hub (cloud, snow on granite, melt, lake, vapour), each a fine line
icon, joined by arrowed arcs. On the right, a few tight modules in the type
voices: the MOUNTAIN_HAIR title over its rule; San Francisco → Tahoe with the
two waters and airs; the bond mask's 30 minutes as the big coral numeral
beside the ACV ratio; the weekly wash's five steps under a dotted section
head; the bracketed state the routine is for. It moves in `steps`, five
beats round the cycle, each lighting its arc and ringing its stage while it
is current.

`scene.ts` is the source; `bun run scenes:bake` writes
`src/content/txt/tahoe-mountain-hair.svg`. Drawn from nothing but the kit:
no external data, no credit.
