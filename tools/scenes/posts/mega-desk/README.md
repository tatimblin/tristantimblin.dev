# mega-desk

The post art for *The Mega Desk*: the drafting sheet — the 8-ft top in iso
on its two telescoping legs, the loft in plan, the leg controller — and the
desk rising from sitting to standing in `steps`, four beats.

`scene.ts` is the source; `bun run scenes:bake` writes
`src/content/txt/mega-desk.svg`. Drawn from nothing but the kit (the hidden
lines are `convexHull` + `clipOutside` from `lib/micro`): no external data,
no credit.
