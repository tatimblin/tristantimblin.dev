# mega-desk

The post art for *The Mega Desk*: the graphic alone — the 8-ft top in iso line
work on its two dual-motor legs, the leg cable slung between them with its
coral Etsy extension, the footprint and the "8 FT" span on the floor,
filling the plate — and the desk rising from sitting to standing in
`steps`, four beats, the still frame standing (always).

`scene.ts` is the source; `bun run scenes:bake` writes
`src/content/txt/mega-desk.svg`. Drawn from nothing but the kit (the hidden
lines are `convexHull` + `clipOutside` from `lib/micro`; the type is the
kit's `text`): no
external data, no credit.
